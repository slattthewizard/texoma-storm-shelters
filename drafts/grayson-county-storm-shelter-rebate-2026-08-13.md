### Understanding FEMA P-320 and ICC-500 Standards

These are the gold standards for safe room construction. FEMA P-320 provides guidance for building residential safe rooms, while ICC-500 is a comprehensive standard for the design and construction of all types of storm shelters. A shelter that meets these criteria is designed to withstand extreme wind events, including EF-5 tornadoes, and protect occupants from debris impact. Key elements include:

*   **Robust Materials:** Heavy-gauge steel, reinforced concrete, or other approved materials.
*   **Secure Anchoring:** Proper anchoring to the foundation to prevent uplift and overturning.
*   **Impact Resistance:** Walls, ceilings, and doors designed to resist penetration from wind-borne debris.
*   **Ventilation:** Adequate airflow to ensure occupant comfort and safety.
*   **Door and Hardware:** Heavy-duty, outward-swinging doors with three points of contact.
