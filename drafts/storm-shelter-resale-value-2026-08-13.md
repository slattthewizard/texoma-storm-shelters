## Quality Matters: FEMA and ICC-500 Standards for Lasting Value

When discussing storm shelter resale value, the quality and certification of the shelter are paramount. A shelter that meets recognized national standards like FEMA P-320 and ICC-500 is not just a marketing point; it is a guarantee of robust protection. These standards are developed through extensive research and testing to ensure shelters can withstand the extreme forces of tornadoes, including high winds and projectile impacts.

**FEMA P-320 (Taking Shelter from the Storm: Building a Safe Room for Your Home or Small Business)** provides guidelines for constructing safe rooms that offer near-absolute protection. It covers design, construction materials, and installation practices. Adherence to these guidelines ensures a shelter's integrity during severe weather events.

**ICC-500 (Standard for the Design and Construction of Storm Shelters)** is a building code standard developed by the International Code Council and the National Storm Shelter Association. It offers more prescriptive requirements for the design, construction, and installation of storm shelters, addressing everything from structural loads to ventilation and accessibility.
