## Underground Storm Shelters and Garage Floor Options

Underground storm shelters offer a traditional and highly effective method of protection. These shelters are buried beneath the ground, typically outside in a yard or partially beneath a patio. Their primary advantage is the earth itself, which provides an additional layer of insulation and protection from direct impacts. They are accessed via a steel door and stairs.

Another popular underground option is the garage floor storm shelter. These are installed directly into your garage floor, typically requiring a concrete cut and excavation. Once installed, the shelter is flush with the garage floor, covered by a heavy-duty, reinforced lid. This design saves yard space and keeps the shelter conveniently located near your home. When closed, you can park your vehicle directly over it.

A family in Pottsboro chose a garage floor storm shelter for $4,500. They liked that it was hidden and didn't take up any yard space. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the key differences between above-ground and underground storm shelters?
Above-ground storm shelters, often called safe rooms, are installed inside a garage or an existing room and are easily accessible without stairs. They are ideal for individuals with mobility concerns. Underground shelters are buried beneath the ground, offering natural insulation and protection, but require stairs for access. Garage floor shelters are a type of underground unit, installed flush with the garage floor.

### Do storm shelters in Denison, TX qualify for rebates or grants?
Yes, residents in Grayson County, including Denison, may qualify for rebates or grants through various federal and state programs like the FEMA Hazard Mitigation Grant Program. These programs aim to offset installation costs. Texoma Storm Shelters can provide documentation to support your application and guide you on where to find information about current programs.

### How long does it take to install a storm shelter?
The installation time for a storm shelter varies by type and site conditions. Above-ground safe rooms can often be installed in a single day. Underground and garage floor shelters, which require excavation and concrete work, typically take 1-3 days. Our team works efficiently and professionally to minimize disruption to your home.

### What FEMA or ICC standards should I look for when buying a storm shelter?
When purchasing a storm shelter, always ensure it is certified to meet FEMA P-320 (Taking Shelter from the Storm: Building a Safe Room for Your Home or Small Business) and ICC 500 (Standard for the Design and Construction of Storm Shelters) standards. These codes specify the design, construction, and impact resistance requirements for shelters to withstand EF5 tornado forces.
